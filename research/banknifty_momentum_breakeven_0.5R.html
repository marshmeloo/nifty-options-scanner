Strategy Research Note · Vol. 2

Bank Nifty Momentum: The Breakeven@0.5R Exit Rule

The same exit logic now live on Bank Nifty — arm a breakeven stop once a trade reaches 0.5R. Five years of real market data, real costs, real measured spread. It earns more than NIFTY, and it hurts considerably more to hold.

Prepared 2026-08-15 Universe Bank Nifty index options Window 2021-08-04 → 2026-08-11 (1,244 trading days, 8,324 trades) Live in production
Executive Summary

Bank Nifty momentum runs the identical pipeline as its NIFTY counterpart — buy calls or puts on an OI/IV/PCR/VWAP signal, 2R target, ATR-derived stop, and since 2026-08-14 a breakeven stop armed once a trade reaches +0.5R. Replayed across 8,324 trades with real statutory costs and real measured spread on every leg, it returns +₹45,73,883 — a +915% cumulative return on a flat ₹5,00,000 base, more than the NIFTY version earns.

It is also a materially rougher instrument to hold. Maximum drawdown is 40.9% (₹2,04,657) against NIFTY's 16.8% — roughly 2.4× the pain for about 1.2× the profit. The trade profile differs in kind, not just degree: 69.6% of Bank Nifty trades simply ride to the end of the day without touching target or stop, and only 8.5% ever reach the 2R target. This note reports those numbers alongside the returns, and is explicit about where its supporting evidence is thinner than the NIFTY note's (§6).

01 — The StrategyIdentical logic, different instrument

The live process (main_live_banknifty.py) is separate from NIFTY's, but imports the same scanner.py, plan_generator.py, risk_checker.py and trade_tracker.py. It differs only in instrument-specific configuration. Nothing in it places a broker order — it is decision-support the operator acts on manually.

A trade closes on the first of four conditions, checked in this order every cycle: 2R target → breakeven-armed stop (reached +0.5R at some point, price now back at entry) → original ATR stop → forced end-of-day close.

02 — Data & Method

Every cycle is reconstructed from Dhan's Expired Options Data endpoint — real recorded OHLC, OI and IV at 5-minute granularity — and replayed through the strategy's own code. Bank Nifty's reconstructed history covers 1,244 days, roughly a year shorter than NIFTY's 1,491, because the backfill begins 2021-08-04.

This dataset was regenerated from a completely fresh scanner run on 2026-08-15, not reloaded from any cached file. It independently reproduced an earlier Bank Nifty run to the rupee on both the baseline and breakeven totals — a genuine reproducibility check, not a re-presentation of the same output.

The full cost stack, stated honestly

The breakeven figure is not "gross minus costs" — it is a different computation, because the rule replaces the outcome of trades that round-tripped. Both are shown so the arithmetic is checkable:

The trade-off in one line
Bank Nifty earns roughly 1.24× NIFTY's profit while inflicting roughly 2.4× the drawdown. Per unit of drawdown endured it is the worse of the two strategies (≈22:1 against NIFTY's ≈44:1), despite the larger headline return. Choosing between them on total P&L alone reads the wrong number.

04 — A Different Kind of StrategyWhy the outcome mix matters

The two indices produce genuinely different trade profiles from identical logic. On Bank Nifty, 69.6% of trades never touch target or stop at all — they are force-closed at 15:15. Median peak favorable excursion is just 0.43R; only 45.7% of trades ever reach 0.5R, and only 8.5% reach the 2R target.

Read plainly: on Bank Nifty this is not primarily a target-hitting strategy. It mostly harvests intraday drift banked at the closing bell. That has a direct consequence for the breakeven rule — it fires on only 11.6% of Bank Nifty trades versus 21.6% on NIFTY, because fewer trades travel far enough to arm it. It still nearly doubles net P&L (₹25.7L → ₹45.7L), but from a smaller base of opportunities.

06 — Robustness, and Where It Is Thin

Three checks pass cleanly. Two that support the NIFTY note have not been run here, and this section says so rather than implying equivalent backing.

Reproducible

Fresh rerun matched to the rupee

Regenerated from scratch on 2026-08-15 and independently reproduced an earlier run's baseline (+₹25,74,308) and breakeven (+₹45,73,883) totals exactly, ruling out a caching or staleness artifact.

No losing year

All 6 calendar years positive

2021 (partial) through 2026 (partial) are each net-positive, including 2025 — the year containing the 40.9% drawdown.

Not a few lucky trades

Top-3 trades = 1.01% of total

Trade-level outlier concentration is low (top-10: 2.97%). But see the day-level caveat below — the trade is not the right unit of risk here.

Not available

Almost no live tracking yet

Bank Nifty's live paper-tracking journal holds 6 trades from a single day (2026-08-14), which lost ₹11,565 on a correlated cluster of PE positions. One session is far too little to confirm or contradict anything. NIFTY's equivalent check used 77 trades across 12 days.

Day-level concentration: the trade-level number is reassuring, and misleading
Top-3 trades are only 1.01% of profit. But the top-5 days are 11.96% of all profit, each a 16–26 trade day, and two of them (2023-02-01, 2026-02-01) are Union Budget days. Because the strategy opens many correlated same-direction positions within a session, its real unit of risk is the day, not the trade. The 2026-08-14 live session showed exactly this shape: six PE positions, all reaching ~0.75R, all reversing together.

Not yet run for Bank Nifty

  • Signal-reversal falsification test. The NIFTY note demonstrates its entry signal carries real information by showing that inverting every signal loses roughly 2.5× more than the forward version makes. That test has not been run on Bank Nifty data. Until it is, this signal's validity here is assumed by analogy, not demonstrated.
  • Live-journal cross-check at meaningful sample size. Requires accumulating live paper-tracking sessions; one day exists so far.

07 — What This Does Not Model

01

Personal income tax

Every figure is pre-tax. F&O profit in India is non-speculative business income taxed at your own slab rate — it cannot be generically baked into a backtest. Reduce every net figure by your effective rate.

02

No real-money execution, and no order-entry delay modeled

Nothing in this system has ever placed a real broker order. Every figure assumes a fill at exactly the price that existed when the signal fired. A real order is punched in seconds later and fills elsewhere — and not symmetrically: stops and breakeven exits fire because price is moving against the position, so a delayed exit systematically fills worse. Since 2026-08-15 the live system records what each contract was priced at one and two cycles after every entry and exit, to measure this from observation. That dataset starts empty and contributes nothing here.

03

The breakeven exit fill assumption

For the 966 trades this rule closed, the exit is modeled at ~entry price while still paying full statutory and spread cost. A real stop on a fast reversal may fill better or worse. The live shadow-tracking system gathers forward evidence on exactly this question.

04

Correlated intraday positions are not risk-capped

The strategy opens many same-direction positions in one session, and the day-level concentration above shows profit depends on them. Nothing in the backtest caps how much of a day's risk sits on one directional view; the live daily-loss breaker (3% of capital) is the only backstop, and it never bound in this backtest.

05

Shorter history than NIFTY

1,244 days from 2021-08-04, versus NIFTY's 1,491 from 2020-08-03. Bank Nifty's record misses the 2020–21 period entirely, so it is tested across fewer distinct market regimes.

06

The between-cycles blind spot

Triggers here are checked at reconstructed 5-minute cycles. Live, entries are scanned every 30s and target/stop/breakeven every 5s. A spike that touches a level and reverses inside one reconstructed bar is invisible here.

07

Non-compounding capital

Every trade is sized against the same flat ₹5,00,000 across all five years. Returns quoted are simple, not compounded.

08 — Status

Live as of 2026-08-14 in main_live_banknifty.py, sharing config.BREAKEVEN_ARM_R = 0.5 and trade_tracker.py with the NIFTY process, with its own separate journals and shadow-tracking files.

How to read this note against the NIFTY one
The Bank Nifty result is larger and less well-supported. It earns more in absolute terms, on a shorter history, with 2.4× the drawdown, a profile that depends on end-of-day drift rather than target hits, meaningful day-level profit concentration, and without the falsification test that backs the NIFTY signal. None of that makes it wrong — but it is a weaker evidential position, and the higher headline return should not be mistaken for higher confidence.
